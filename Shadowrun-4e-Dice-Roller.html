Rules used
  • A hit is a 5 or a 6.
  • A glitch is a 1.
  • Glitch: half of the dice are glitches. Critical Glitch: half of the dice are glitches and there are 0 hits.
  • Push the Limit spends 1 Edge and adds your Edge rating in dice. Every 6 is rerolled and the new die counts too, repeating. Exploded dice are not counted for glitches.
  • Second Chance spends 1 Edge and rerolls every die that is not a hit. It is not available after Push the Limit.
